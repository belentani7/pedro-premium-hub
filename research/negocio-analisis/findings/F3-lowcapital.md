# F3: Low-Capital Business Analysis ($0-$5,000)

**Research Date**: 2026-07-22  
**Scope**: Business models with startup costs under $5,000  
**Methodology**: Web research from multiple business resources (2024-2026 data)

---

## Executive Summary

The median startup cost for businesses with no employees is $5,000 (Kauffman Foundation). Service-based businesses generate income fastest (1-4 weeks), while digital models take longer (1-6 months) but scale better. Competition is highest in dropshipping and affiliate marketing; lowest in specialized consulting and local services.

## 11. KEY INSIGHTS

### Fastest Path to Revenue (Under $500)
1. Pet Sitting/Dog Walking (1-2 weeks)
2. House Cleaning (1-3 weeks)
3. Freelance Writing/VA (2-6 weeks)

### Highest Scalability
1. Digital Products & Courses (global, zero marginal cost)
2. Affiliate Marketing (passive income potential)
3. Cleaning/Bookkeeping Agencies (recurring contracts + employees)

### Lowest Competition (Highest Opportunity)
1. Handyman Services (reliability-focused, low competition)
2. Bookkeeping (sticky clients, low churn)
3. Niche Digital Marketing (specificity wins)

### Highest Risk (Competition + Slow Revenue)
1. Affiliate Marketing (very high competition, 2-6 months to revenue)
2. Dropshipping (very high competition, hidden costs)
3. Blogging (very high competition, 3-6 months minimum)
