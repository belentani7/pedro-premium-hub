# Digital Business Models with Recurring Revenue: Benchmarks & Analysis

## 1. SaaS (Niche/Vertical)

- **Date:** 2025
- **Confidence:** High

**Claim:** Median LTV:CAC ratio for private B2B SaaS companies is 3.6:1 (2024), with 3:1 as the floor, not the target.

- **Date:** 2024-2025
- **Confidence:** High

**Claim:** Median New CAC Ratio increased 14% in 2024 to $2.00 per $1 of new ARR.

- **Date:** 2024-2025
- **Confidence:** High

**Claim:** Median B2B SaaS Net Revenue Retention (NRR) is 82% (2025), significantly below the commonly cited 100% threshold.

- **Confidence:** High

**Claim:** Companies with NRR above 100% grow at 48% YoY, double the rate of those below 100%.

- **Date:** 2025
- **Confidence:** High

**Claim:** Gross Revenue Retention (GRR) slipped to 88% in 2025 (down from 90%).
- **Source:** https://www.benchmarkit.ai/2025benchmarks
- **Date:** 2025
- **Confidence:** High

**Claim:** Median SaaS Gross Margin is 71-72% (public SaaS, Q1 2025).

- **Date:** 2025
- **Confidence:** Medium

**Claim:** Best-in-class SaaS companies reach $10M ARR in 2 years and 9 months; median takes just over 5 years.

- **Date:** 2025
- **Confidence:** High

**Claim:** Only 11-30% of SaaS companies meet the Rule of 40 threshold (growth rate + profit margin ≥ 40).

- **Date:** 2025
- **Confidence:** High

**Claim:** Usage-based billing delivers 10% higher NRR, 22% lower churn, and 2x faster growth than fixed subscription models.

**Claim:** AI-native SaaS companies show dramatically lower retention (GRR 40% in Sept 2025) vs traditional B2B SaaS (88%).

## Key Comparative Insights

**Claim:** CAC efficiency has deteriorated across all models: SaaS CAC ratio up 14% in 2024; subscription boxes face rising ad costs.
- **Source:** Benchmarkit 2025, Zipdo 2026
- **Date:** 2024-2026
- **Confidence:** High

**Claim:** Retention is the primary growth lever: Companies with NRR >100% grow 2x faster; subscription boxes with churn <5% achieve 5:1 LTV:CAC.
- **Source:** ChartMogul 2025, SubscriptionBoxCalculator 2026
- **Date:** 2025-2026
- **Confidence:** High

**Claim:** Profitability timelines vary: SaaS companies often cash-flow negative for 20+ months; subscription boxes can be profitable at 50-150 subscribers; online courses can achieve payback in under 12 months with right metrics.
- **Source:** Benchmarkit 2025, SubscriptionBoxCalculator 2026, Passion.io 2025
- **Date:** 2025-2026
- **Confidence:** High

**Claim:** Gross margin targets: SaaS 75-80%+, Subscription Boxes 40-55%, Digital Education 70%+ (high due to low COGS).
- **Source:** Blossom Street 2025, SubscriptionBoxCalculator 2026, LiveSkillsHub 2025
- **Date:** 2025-2026
- **Confidence:** High
