# F5: Digital vs Physical Businesses — Comprehensive Comparative Analysis

**Date compiled:** July 2025
**Methodology:** Multi-source web research, cross-referenced from industry reports, comparison guides, and startup surveys.

---

| Claim | Source | Date | Confidence |
|-------|--------|------|------------|

| Physical companies require investment proportional to growth: more premises, more staff, more inventory | JLC Auditors analysis | 2025-04 | High |
| Digital scales fast but fragile; retail scales slower but more defensible | Hanifoundation.org | 2025-12 | High |
| Online businesses have structural scalability advantage: adding 100th SaaS customer costs nearly nothing | ByeCorporate comparison guide | 2025-12 | High |
| Physical businesses scale through replication — opening more locations requires capital, local management, and execution risk | ByeCorporate comparison guide | 2025-12 | High |
| Physical businesses can dominate local market more completely (local plumber vs global competition) | ByeCorporate comparison guide | 2025-12 | Medium |

However, physical businesses have a defensibility advantage through local market dominance and harder-to-replicate experiences.

| Claim | Source | Date | Confidence |
|-------|--------|------|------------|

| Retail risks: lease commitments, inventory management, labor issues | Hanifoundation.org | 2025-12 | High |
| Online risk is invisible but constant; retail risk is visible but manageable | Hanifoundation.org | 2025-12 | High |
| Physical business failure: can lose savings, home equity, years of life | ByeCorporate comparison guide | 2025-12 | High |
| Digital business failure: lose time and a few thousand dollars | ByeCorporate comparison guide | 2025-12 | High |
| Physical businesses highly vulnerable to external disruption (pandemic lockdowns) | ByeCorporate comparison guide | 2025-12 | High |
| Undercapitalization is leading cause of early business failure for physical businesses | ByeCorporate comparison guide | 2025-12 | High |
