## 6. ROOT CAUSE HIERARCHY (Ranked by frequency)

From composite analysis of CB Insights (431 companies), Failory (80+ founders), Exploding Topics:

| Rank | Root Cause | Frequency | Source |
|------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| 1 | Poor product-market fit | 34-43% | CB Insights, Failory |
| 2 | Bad timing / macro conditions | 29% | CB Insights |
| 3 | Unsustainable unit economics | 19% | CB Insights |
| 4 | Team knowledge gaps | 18% | Failory |
| 5 | Financial / cash flow issues | 16% | Failory |
| 6 | Technical problems | 6% | Failory |
| 7 | Operations problems | 2% | Failory |
| 8 | Legal problems | 2% | Failory |

**Key insight**: Running out of capital (70%) is the FINAL cause of death in almost every case, but almost never the ROOT cause. The root causes are PMF, timing, and unit economics.

## 7. STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS FOR NEW BUSINESS ENTRY

### Markets to AVOID (saturated/high-failure):
1. Healthcare/Biotech without deep domain expertise and $50M+ capital
2. Alt-protein/cultivated meat — wave is unwinding
3. Climate/energy ventures dependent on policy that hasn't materialized
4. Consumer blockchain/crypto without clear utility
5. Robotics (99% failure rate)
6. AI without a specific, monetizable vertical application

### Markets with OPPORTUNITY (underserved/growing):
1. Vertical AI SaaS for trades and local services (42% CAGR)
2. Pet economy digital products (67% growth, underserved)
3. AI consulting for small businesses (78% adoption creating training gap)
4. Non-alcoholic beverages ($1.48T market, 8% CAGR)
5. Healthcare IT (14.7% CAGR, $420B→$834B)
6. Fashion resale (127% projected growth by 2026)
7. Local service businesses (highest survival rates)
8. Compliance/governance tools (DSA/GDPR enforcement)

### Critical Success Factors:
1. Validate product-market fit BEFORE scaling (65% die from premature scaling)
2. Pivot 1-2 times maximum (3.6x better outcomes)
3. Allow 2-3x more time for market validation than planned
4. Focus on marketing/distribution early (56% die from marketing failure)
5. Don't confuse running out of cash with the actual problem — fix PMF, timing, or unit economics first
