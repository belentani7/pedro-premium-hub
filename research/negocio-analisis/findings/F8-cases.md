## FAILURE STORIES

### 1. FTX (Cryptocurrency Exchange)
- **Claim**: FTX collapsed in November 2022 after a bank run, owing $8 billion to creditors, with founder Sam Bankman-Fried convicted of fraud in 2024.
- **Source**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FTX_(company)
- **Date**: November 2022
- **Root Causes**:
  - Misuse of customer funds: Lent $10 billion of customer assets to Alameda Research
  - Conflict of interest: Alameda Research had secret exemption from liquidation protocols
  - Poor governance: No board oversight, limited financial controls
  - Fraud: Used software to conceal misuse of customer funds
  - Contagion: Triggered collapse of BlockFi, Celsius, and other crypto firms
- **Confidence**: High (99%)

### 2. WeWork (Coworking Spaces)
- **Claim**: WeWork filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in November 2023 after burning through $12.8 billion in funding, with liabilities of $10-50 billion.
- **Source**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WeWork
- **Date**: November 2023
- **Root Causes**:
  - Overvaluation: Valued at $47 billion but never profitable
  - Corporate governance: Founder Adam Neumann's excessive spending and conflicts
  - Business model flaw: Long-term leases vs. short-term memberships
  - Failed IPO: 2019 IPO attempt revealed governance issues
  - COVID-19 impact: Remote work reduced demand for coworking spaces
- **Confidence**: High (99%)

### 3. Bed Bath & Beyond (Retail)
- **Claim**: Bed Bath & Beyond filed for bankruptcy in April 2023 after failing to compete with Amazon and other e-commerce retailers.
- **Source**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bed_Bath_%26_Beyond
- **Date**: April 2023
- **Root Causes**:
  - E-commerce disruption: Lost market share to Amazon, Walmart, Target
  - Failed turnaround attempts: Hired and fired multiple CEOs
  - Inventory mismanagement: Stocked wrong products for changing consumer preferences
  - Financial distress: Accumulated debt while trying to compete
  - Meme stock bubble: Brief surge in stock price distracted from core problems
- **Confidence**: High (95%)

### 4. Silicon Valley Bank (Banking)
- **Claim**: SVB collapsed in March 2023 after a bank run, becoming the second-largest bank failure in U.S. history with $209 billion in assets.
- **Source**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon_Valley_Bank
- **Date**: March 2023
- **Root Causes**:
  - Interest rate risk: Heavy investment in long-term Treasury bonds during low rates
  - Concentration risk: 85%+ of deposits from tech startups and VCs
  - Social media amplification: VC tweets triggered mass withdrawal panic
  - Liquidity mismatch: Long-term assets vs. short-term deposit obligations
  - Regulatory gaps: Failed to hedge interest rate risk adequately
- **Confidence**: High (99%)

### 5. Celsius Network (Crypto Lending)
- **Claim**: Celsius Network paused withdrawals in June 2022 and filed for bankruptcy in July 2022, owing $4.7 billion to users.
- **Source**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celsius_Network
- **Date**: July 2022
- **Root Causes**:
  - Ponzi-like structure: Used new deposits to pay existing users' yields
  - Re-hypothecation: Lent the same assets repeatedly to boost yields
  - Market risk: Heavy exposure to Terra/LUNA collapse
  - Fraud: CEO Alex Mashinsky manipulated CEL token price
  - Regulatory evasion: Ignored cease-and-desist orders from multiple states
- **Confidence**: High (99%)

## COMMON PATTERNS

### Success Patterns
1. **Product-Market Fit**: All successful companies solved real problems at scale
2. **Platform Strategy**: Most built platforms that attracted ecosystem participants
3. **AI Integration**: 2023-2026 winners aggressively adopted AI features
4. **Community Building**: Discord and Canva grew through user communities
5. **Strategic Partnerships**: OpenAI-Microsoft, Stripe-Shopify, Notion-integrations

### Failure Patterns
1. **Governance Failures**: FTX, WeWork, Celsius all had weak board oversight
2. **Overleverage**: WeWork's long-term leases, SVB's bond portfolio, FTX's margin lending
3. **Fraud/Deception**: FTX and Celsius involved deliberate misrepresentation
4. **Concentration Risk**: SVB's tech-focused deposits, Celsius's crypto-only model
5. **Regulatory Ignorance**: All failed companies ignored or evaded regulations

### Pivot Stories
1. **Discord**: Started as a gaming communication tool, pivoted to general community platform
2. **Canva**: Initially focused on social media graphics, expanded to enterprise design suite
3. **Notion**: Pivoted from note-taking app to all-in-one workspace and AI platform
4. **Stripe**: Expanded from payment processing to full financial infrastructure

---

## KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR ENTREPRENEURS

1. **Timing Matters**: OpenAI's ChatGPT launch coincided with AI breakthrough moment
2. **Governance is Non-Negotiable**: Every failed company had governance failures
3. **Diversify Risk**: Don't concentrate exposure to single sector, rate environment, or customer type
4. **Build for Scale Early**: Stripe and Canva designed for millions of users from day one
5. **Embrace AI**: 2023-2026 winners integrated AI; those who didn't fell behind
6. **Regulatory Compliance**: Ignoring regulators creates existential risk
7. **Community > Features**: Discord and Canva grew through communities, not just features
